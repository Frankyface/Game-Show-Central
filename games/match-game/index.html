            Prompts 
            
          
            One prompt per line. Every one needs a blank written as
            ___ and must be 160 characters or shorter. Thirty is the
            minimum; sixty gives a whole night. A hint goes after a vertical bar:
            Dizzy Daisy ate a ___. | food
